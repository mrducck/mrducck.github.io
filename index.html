Game · 2026

Reefed

Cottagecore meets reef exploration. Fish, dig, farm and cook at home, then take a boat out where the tide makes things dangerous. I design the systems, build the models and UI, and write the plugins.

  • Fishing loot that changes with climate and water type
  • 28 boats plus premium skins, the main coin sink in the economy
  • Tide nights: a nightly roll that raises both danger and rewards
  • Java and Bedrock crossplay from one resource pack
  • A Monte Carlo sim of the first four hours, used to tune the economy
